# Basketball Manager 26 privacy policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Developer: Roman Divkovic

This policy applies to the Unity macOS and iOS game **Basketball Manager 26**, bundle
identifier `com.romandivkovic.basketballmanager`, version 1.0.0.

## Data on your device

The game stores local account usernames, salted password hashes, recovery-code
hashes, coach profiles, league databases and career saves on your device. Your
password is not stored as plain text. CSV files that you choose to import are
copied into the game's local storage so they can be used for your league.

The Mac App Store edition uses the macOS App Sandbox. On iOS, the game uses
the system app sandbox. Accounts, saves and imported data remain in the app's
local data container. This version has no
cloud save or cross-device account service.

## Data collection and sharing

The App Store editions do not transmit game accounts, passwords, league
files, career saves or usage analytics to the developer or third-party partners.
The macOS edition does not request a network entitlement. Advertising, Unity Analytics and
cloud crash reporting are disabled. The game contains no advertising or
in-app purchases.

Apple processes App Store purchases, downloads and any information you choose
to share with Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your choices

You can delete a local account and its associated careers from the game's
account settings. Imported CSV copies or other cached files can remain in the
local data container. Removing the entire app data container removes the
remaining local game data. Keep a separate backup if you want to retain your
careers; deleting local data cannot be reversed by the developer.

Your device's backup and device-management settings may separately control copies
of local app data. These are not a cloud save service provided by the game.

## Support

For support or questions about this policy, use the project's
[support page](SUPPORT_MAC_APP_STORE.md). If you submit a public GitHub issue,
the information you choose to include is visible publicly and is processed
under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes

If a later version introduces online features or changes these practices, this
policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated for that version.
